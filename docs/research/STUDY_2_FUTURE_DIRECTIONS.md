# Study 2 / Future Master's Research Directions

Study 1 validates the research apparatus under deterministic paired controls. The next research programme should increase ecological and causal validity rather than adding unrelated product features.

## Priority 1 — Source-level mutation campaign

Create independently reviewable compiler mutants at known boundaries: requirements extraction, foundation planning, capability composition, SML rendering/conformance, SIR transformation, and target generation. Run the frozen SSM-Bench v2 against each mutant and measure detection sensitivity, false negatives, attribution accuracy, and slice localisation.

## Priority 2 — Online provider variance under fixed canonical semantics

Hold `input.md` and CanonicalSemanticContext constant while repeating online candidate-SML synthesis across providers/models and prompt versions. Measure candidate-SML variance, repair burden, conformance rejection rate, accepted semantic equivalence, cost, latency, and downstream generated-tree stability.

## Priority 3 — Negation and ambiguity research

Build a held-out requirements benchmark concentrating on negation, scope, precedence, coreference, contradictory stakeholder statements, incomplete specifications, and domain-word collisions. Compare deterministic extraction with constrained model-assisted interpretation while preserving explicit uncertainty rather than silently resolving it.

## Priority 4 — Independent oracle adjudication

Have at least two independent annotators label semantic obligations for a future benchmark version. Measure agreement, adjudicate disagreements, and separate benchmark-label uncertainty from compiler error.

## Priority 5 — Sequential evolution monitoring

Estimate an offline noise floor from repeated release runs, then evaluate sequential/anytime-valid monitoring for provider drift and production-quality changes. Preserve `INCONCLUSIVE` as a first-class state.

## Priority 6 — Larger held-out corpus

Create SSM-Bench v3 as a new content-addressed corpus with longer multi-section PRDs, multiple domains, integration-heavy applications, more NFRs, and unseen writing styles. Keep v2 immutable for longitudinal comparability.
